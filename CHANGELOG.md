# What's new in Quill

Newest first. Each "##" heading is one update. Quill shows the updates you haven't seen yet, once per device, the first time you open it after an update.

## Sep 22 · Prompts, search and typewriter mode
- **Writing prompts.** Tap the ✦ button in any group for a new sheet that starts with a prompt, tucked in a comment so it doesn't count as words. Quill never gives you the same prompt twice, on any device.
- **Your prompt list.** "Prompts" in the Library shows how many are left and opens the whole list, so you can add your own (one per line, starting with "- "). Quill started it with 230 of its own.
- **Search** everything with the magnifying glass at the top of the Library, or ⌘F. It looks through your sheets and their notes.
- **All** and **Last 7 Days** in the Library show sheets from every group at once.
- **Typewriter mode** keeps the line you're typing in the middle of the screen. Turn it on in the ••• menu (Mac and iPad).
- **This pop-up.** From now on, each update tells you what changed. You can read it again in Settings.
- Fixed: a sheet started on one device stayed "Untitled …" in Dropbox if you finished it on another. Now whichever device you leave it on names it from its first line (once). Your Backroom sheet is now "The Backroom of the Shop".

## Sep 21 · Folding notes and adjustable columns
- Fold a long note down to one line with the arrow beside its heading.
- Drag the dividers between columns to resize them. Double-click a divider to reset it.
- The button at the top left of the editor now steps through all columns, without the Library, and just the text.
- On iPhone, Quill opens on the Library unless you left in the middle of a sheet.
- Fixed: deleting a note could leave another note's title selected, ready to be typed over.
- Fixed: notes written for a new sheet that was left empty were left behind. They now go to Trash.

## Sep 21 · Your quill icon
- The home-screen icon is now your quill. To see it, remove Quill from the home screen and add it again (check "All changes synced" first).

## Sep 21 · Select a paragraph with ⌘L
- ⌘L selects the paragraph you're in. Press it again to add the next one.

## Sep 21 · Notes, comments and selected-word count
- Notes for sheets and groups, with the paperclip.
- Comments (`<!-- like this -->`) stay out of the word count and titles. Use the speech-bubble button or ⌘/.
- With text selected, the word count shows "12 of 1,244 words".

## Sep 21 · The first real Quill
- Library, sheet list and editor, laid out for Mac, iPad and iPhone.
- Writing works offline and syncs through Dropbox, keeping both versions if a sheet changes on two devices.
- Trash, Put back, word count, focus mode and dark mode.
